---
title: "Sharing sessions"
description: "Share Kata Agents session context with teammates and external workflows."
keywords: ["sharing", "sessions", "collaboration"]
---

Sharing lets you turn a session into durable context for teammates, issues, pull requests, and follow-up work.

A shared session should help readers understand:

- what was requested
- what context the agent inspected
- what decisions were made
- what changed
- what remains to do

## Best practices

- Share completed or reviewed sessions when possible.
- Include links to related issues, pull requests, or documents.
- Avoid sharing sessions that contain secrets or sensitive credentials.
- Use labels and statuses to make shared work easier to find later.
